You are writing one realistic conversation for a research dataset: a person chatting with a general-purpose AI assistant. Each turn is one message from the person and one reply from the assistant.

# Situation
- Topic: a team tryout or club. The angle for this conversation: being picked last.
- What the person wants from the chat: a check on whether their own idea or attempt is right.
- First write `scenario`: one or two sentences on this person's specific, slightly unusual situation (not the textbook example for the topic). It is only for planning and is never shown, so it may say who the person is.

# The person
- Temperament: Chatty. They share side details and small stories about their situation.
- Message length: One or two sentences, at most 25 words. The first message may run a little longer, because it sets out the situation.
- They write the way people really type to a chatbot: plain everyday words, contractions, no em dashes, no lists, no formal sign-offs. Use normal spelling, capitals and apostrophes; do not imitate typos.
- Before turn 4 the wording is plain enough to sound natural from a 13-year-old or a 45-year-old. From turn 4 on they sound more like who they are, the way people loosen up once a chat is going: they think in goals, limits and what each option costs (time, money, effort, what they already tried), give the context up front, hedge a little, and check specifics in the answer against their own case. No essay and no corporate jargon. Every message is worded freshly; no stock phrases. It is the same person throughout, with no sudden change of personality.
- They react to what the assistant just said (skim it, pick up one part, push back, go off on a small tangent), bring something new each turn, and do not wrap up or say goodbye before the last turn.
- If they name anyone, they use one of these names: Jonah, Sam, Hana.

# The assistant

- It never guesses or hints at the person's age or stage of life, and it never brings up school, parents, family, work, a partner or children unless the person has already mentioned that same thing.

It still never states or guesses an age the person has not given.
- It is an AI and speaks as one.
